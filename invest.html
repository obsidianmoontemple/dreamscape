What it is

Two engines, one world.

SomnuMatrix (The Dreamer's Atlas) maps the inner world and the shared one. Each night a dreamer tells a dream; it becomes a place. Beyond every private dreamscape stands Somnucor, a city every dreamer shares, live, around the clock.

Engine one · The dream journal

Speech or text, mapped into 3D

Say it the way you would tell a friend. The world builds behind the words as you speak: the street, the castle above the lake, the stranger on the bridge, the weather and the hour.

  • Builds on the device from its own catalogue of 53,000+ words, with no network needed
  • Understands sizes, colours and places: "two blocks down, on the opposite side"
  • A word it has never met is grown into a new form and learnt for every dreamer
  • Each night is sealed with a fingerprint, so later changes show; sync is encrypted with a phrase only the dreamer knows
Engine two · Somnucor

A 24/7 real-time society

A great stepped city of nine rings, shared by every player in real time, with its own clock, laws, economy and institutions. People hold jobs, pay bills, buy homes, stand trial and celebrate festivals together.

  • 220+ city jobs with real shifts and real tasks at real stations
  • Courts, police, a gaol, a bank, a treasury and a land office
  • Hidden worlds: the North Pole, Neverland, the Endless Sea, the Underdark, the Moon
  • Run from the Keeper's Desk, a full admin console with two-factor sign-in
One of a kind

Innovations you will not find anywhere else.

An offline dream-to-world engine

Most AI world builders need a server for every word. Here the first, instant build happens on the device from a 53,000-word catalogue, so the journal works on a plane, in a cabin, at 3 a.m.

A world that learns its vocabulary

When one dreamer names something new, it is built at once and saved to the shared catalogue. The next dreamer who says it finds it already there.

Gods who answer, in character

275 deities from the world's traditions have temples and priests. Speak to a god and the AI answers live and in that god's own voice, for every visitor.

A complete civic system

No punishment without a trial: arrests, charges, judge, prosecutor, public defender and jury, with sentences carried out by the city itself.

Everyone has a place and an address

Every dreamed thing gets a real lot, a name and an address. Every city building has staff at their posts inside, working their hours.

A living timetable

The city keeps its own clock. Signed-off players' avatars go to work, walk home and stay home on schedule; festivals, parades and seasonal events arrive on time.

A shared economy with real rules

Gold, wages, land, sentences and rulings can only change through server-side functions. No player can cheat the economy from the browser.

A tamper-evident dream record

Every night is stored with a cryptographic fingerprint and optional, opt-in research summaries, which makes the journal unusual as a research instrument.

The AI

It reads the dream, looks things up, and builds what it means.

Two layers work together, so the product is fast, cheap to run and still smart.

i

Instant, on the deviceEvery sentence is read as it arrives and built from the offline catalogue. Nothing leaves the phone.

ii

Live researchNames and words it only guessed are looked up on Wikipedia and Wiktionary through a secure server relay.

iii

A language model designs the restA large model (via Groq) returns new shapes, people, weather and mood, merged into the scene the dreamer is already standing in.

iv

Characters with a voiceThe same relay powers figures you can talk to and the deities in the temples. The AI key never reaches the browser.

Serious engineering under the dream.

Phone and PC

Runs in the browser on phones and desktop computers, with touch joystick, on-screen controls, a phone-tuned graphics path and memory checks that run on every change.

Offline-first

The dream engine works with no connection. A service worker keeps models and pictures on the device after the first visit, like a phone game.

Secure by design

Purchases are recorded only by a signed Stripe webhook and checked on the server. Row-level security on every table, two-factor sign-in for keepers, AES-256 encrypted journal sync, AI keys kept server-side.

Stack: HTML and JavaScript, three.js, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth with two-factor, Edge Functions), Groq, Stripe Checkout, browser speech recognition. Four server functions: the AI relay, the Stripe webhook, a bank link and a cinema partner link.

Revenue engines already built in

The money systems are already wired.

You do not have to build commerce. These engines exist in the code today and are run from the Keeper's Desk. Payments go through Stripe, set up by the new owner with their own account.

Purchase and membership

Stripe Checkout sells the product and city membership (dues). A signed webhook records each purchase, and the server checks access on every paid feature. Price, link and support email are set from the admin page; dated plans are supported.

Built · switch on with your Stripe keys

World Mall store units

Outside sellers apply for a unit, design a 3D shopfront and load real products by form, spreadsheet or API key, with approvals on the Keeper's Desk. An embeddable shelf puts their mall products on their own websites and links buyers back in.

Built · seller portal and embed widget

Gold economy and bank

Wages, taxes, a treasury and a bank keep gold moving. An optional exchange can sell and buy gold for real money, settled by hand or through a bank-link API, behind a master switch and two-factor checks.

Built · off by default, needs legal advice

Cosmetics and items

The Dreamskin Atelier sells 36 skins; the Lightworks sells lights to dress homes; hats, relics and gear go to every player's inventory. The catalogue and prices are run from the admin page.

Built · in-world shops
