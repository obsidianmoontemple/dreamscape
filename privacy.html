Privacy

In plain words, and short on purpose.

The short version. This page collects an email address if you choose to give one, so we can tell you when the Atlas opens. Your dreams are a separate matter entirely: they are kept on your own device, and we cannot read them.

What this page collects

If you join the list, we keep: your email address; your name, if you gave one; which edition interested you and how often you remember your dreams, if you answered; and a note of how you arrived here — the advert, link or site that brought you. Nothing else. No account, no password, no tracking across other sites.

What we do with it

We write to you about the Atlas: when the campaign opens, how the work is going, and when it ships. That is all. We do not sell the list, rent it, or pass it to anyone outside the work of launching the Atlas. People helping us run the campaign may handle it under instruction, and only for that.

Leaving

Every letter has a link to leave, and you can also take your address off the list here. It is deleted, not marked inactive. Nothing of yours is kept afterwards.

Your dreams

The Atlas keeps what you write on the device you write it on. We do not receive it, cannot read it, and have built the thing so that it stays that way. There is an optional, anonymous research programme that never carries an account and is off unless you turn it on. The app counts how many devices use it, under a random code with nothing attached, and you can switch that off too — which erases the device from the count.

Where it is kept

The list lives in a database with access rules that keep it closed: it can be added to from this page, but only an administrator signed in to the Temple's desk can read it.

Children

This page is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect email addresses from anyone under 16.

Asking us

For a copy of what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to the Temple. We will answer, and do it.
