Somnucor — the shared city

Somnucor is a city every dreamer shares: work, wages, a bank, ground to hold, a hall where every dreamer has a door, and Temple Row. It is entirely optional. The Atlas works exactly as it does without it, your own dreamscape stays yours either way, and nothing you dream is sent anywhere by joining.

Your work and your purse

Somnucor is the city every dreamer shares. Gold is its money — 100 gold is worth a dollar — and the city's books are kept on the server, not here. Take work, and two months' starting income comes with it.

Sign in above to work, earn and hold ground in Somnucor.

The bank

The bank lends against what you have earned in wages, and never beyond it. Your starting income stays with you and cannot be handed to anybody.

The quarry and the stand

What comes up is yours. The city buys it at a standing price, and what gets built in Somnucor is built out of it.

The market

Dreamers buying and selling to each other, at whatever they ask — usually better than the city's own floor price, since the city takes a small toll on what changes hands here.

A business of your own

Open one, and post the work Somnucor needs done under it. Opening one costs gold; posting work and hiring who applies doesn't.

Your door in the great hall

Somnucor is the hall every dreamer arrives in. Take a door and it joins the hall, however many there come to be. A door carries only what you write on it.

Letting people in

Your door only opens if you say so, and only onto a copy you publish. That copy is not your vault — the vault stays locked and unreadable. Visitors can walk your world and change nothing.
