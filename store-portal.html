Import a list

Paste a spreadsheet saved as CSV, or choose the file. The first row names the columns: sku, name, description, image_url, price_gold, real_price, buy_url. Only sku and name are required; a matching code updates that product.

Connect your website to the World Mall

Three ways, from the simplest. Copy and paste the code into your website wherever you want it to appear — most site builders (Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, WordPress) have a “Custom HTML” or “Embed code” block for exactly this.

These go live the moment your unit is granted. You can paste them now; until then they simply show nothing.
